# CalcExpress privacy policy

_Last updated: 21 October 2026_

CalcExpress does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
- Calculations, history and settings stay on your device.
- **GitHub version only:** to look for updates, the app sends an anonymous request to
  `api.github.com` (GitHub's public Releases API) and, if you choose to update, downloads the APK from
  GitHub. GitHub may log standard request data (such as IP address) under
  [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
  You can turn off the automatic check in Settings → About the app.
- **Google Play version:** the app has no internet permission at all.

Permissions (GitHub version): `INTERNET` (update check/download) and `REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES`
(lets you install the downloaded update; Android always asks you to confirm).

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/ArturPelicho/calcexpress/issues
